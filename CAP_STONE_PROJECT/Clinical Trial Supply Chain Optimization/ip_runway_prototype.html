Agentic AI Capstone · Pharmaceutical Supply Chain

IP Runway

A phase-by-phase agentic prototype that tells a clinical supply manager which trial sites will run out of investigational product within 30 days, why, and what to review before it happens.

⚠ Synthetic prototype data only — decision support, not connected to any ERP, IRT/RTSM, or CTMS. No real patient data.

Deliverable 1

Business problem & objective

Phase II and Phase III trials run across many sites with uncertain enrollment, dropout, protocol amendments, expiry limits, and variable consumption. Reactive supply planning turns that uncertainty into stockouts, delayed treatment, emergency shipments, wastage, and higher trial cost.

The prototype exists to answer one question on demand: “Which sites are at risk within 30 days, why, and what action should a supply manager review?”

Every recommendation is evidence-backed, confidence-scored, and routed for human approval when risk or transfer size crosses a threshold — the agent assesses and recommends, it never releases stock or edits a system of record.

Primary users

  • Clinical Supply Manager — reviews risk, approves replenishment
  • Global Trial Supply Planner — monitors demand and cross-site allocation

Secondary users

  • Clinical Operations Manager — patient/study impact review
  • Logistics Coordinator — shipment feasibility
  • Quality/Compliance Reviewer — audit readiness

Deliverable 2 & 6

Architecture, orchestrator & sub-agents

One orchestrator validates every request and hands it down a fixed pipeline of specialist agents, each exchanging typed objects rather than free-text.

flowchart TD
  U[Supply Manager / Planner request] --> ORC[Orchestrator]
  ORC --> GR1{Pre-request guardrail}
  GR1 -- fail --> BLOCK[Blocked: unauthorized or malformed]
  GR1 -- pass --> DA[Demand Agent]
  DA --> IA[Inventory Agent]
  IA --> RA[Risk Agent]
  RA --> CA[Compliance Agent]
  CA -- HIGH / CRITICAL or large transfer --> AG{Human approval gate}
  CA -- low impact, no flag --> REP[Reporting Agent]
  AG -- approved --> REP
  AG -- rejected --> REV[Returned for revision]
  REP --> OUT[Evidence-backed report]
  MCP1[(MCP: enrollment)] --- DA
  MCP2[(MCP: inventory / expiry)] --- IA
  MCP3[(MCP: shipments)] --- IA
  MCP4[(MCP: protocol / SOP docs)] --- CA
  AUD[[Audit & trace log]] --- ORC
  AUD --- CA
  AUD --- AG
Orchestrator

Validates the request, checks authorization scope, builds the plan, and controls every state transition.

Demand Agent

Calculates expected kit consumption over the horizon from enrollment, dropout, and dosing schedule.

Inventory Agent

Calculates usable stock after safety reserve, expiry exposure, and incoming shipments due in-horizon.

Risk Agent

Classifies LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL against fixed thresholds and drafts a replenishment quantity.

Compliance Agent

Checks evidence completeness, authorization, prohibited actions, and whether human approval is required.

Reporting Agent

Formats the final business output from approved state only — never from an intermediate or rejected draft.

    Deliverable 8

    State, context & memory

    Every run carries one shared workflow state object. Agents receive only the fields their role needs — context isolation, not a shared free-for-all.

    {
  "request_id": "REQ-0147",
  "study_id": "PH3-ONC-2027",
  "site_id": "IND002",
  "current_step": "risk_assessment",
  "plan": ["demand", "inventory", "risk", "compliance", "approval", "report"],
  "agent_outputs": { "...": "..." },
  "evidence": ["enrollment-IND002-20260925", "inventory-IND002-20260925"],
  "guardrail_results": [{ "check": "AUTH_SCOPE", "status": "PASS" }],
  "approval": { "status": "PENDING" },
  "final_status": "IN_PROGRESS"
}

    Short-term: request, plan, tool responses, intermediate outputs, and approval state for one run. Long-term: versioned historical forecasts, outcomes, and approvals under non-sensitive identifiers — the Session audit trail in Live prototype demonstrates this in-browser, per viewer. Conflict rule: current approved source data always overrides memory, and the conflict is logged.

    Deliverable 9

    Guardrails & human-in-the-loop

    Guardrails fail closed: on missing, conflicting, stale, or under-threshold evidence, the run stops and escalates rather than guessing. HIGH/CRITICAL risk, possible patient impact, or a transfer above the configured threshold cannot proceed without a recorded human decision — and a rejection ends the path, it is never silently bypassed.

    See it enforced live — three of the six prototype sites are built to trip a specific guardrail:

    Enforced automatically

    • Least privilege, RBAC, read-only tool access
    • Evidence completeness & freshness
    • Authorization scope per requesting user

    Routed to a human

    • HIGH / CRITICAL risk classification
    • Stale evidence, regardless of risk level
    • Transfer quantity above the configured threshold

    Deliverable 10

    AI governance approach

    • Least privilege, role-based access, and data minimization on every tool call.
    • Every material claim carries a source-evidence ID — no invented facts, no concealed uncertainty.
    • Deterministic calculation is kept separate from LLM-generated explanation, so the numbers never depend on model phrasing.
    • Model/prompt version and the accountable human decision-maker are recorded on every approval.
    • Risk is never inferred from geography, demographics, or other protected characteristics.
    • Output is decision support only — the agent recommends for review, it never releases, cancels, or authorizes a shipment.
